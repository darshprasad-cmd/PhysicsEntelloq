THREE IDEAS WORTH KEEPING

Understand the motion beneath the machine.

01

Geometry asks “where?”

Forward kinematics turns joint angles into a tool position. Inverse kinematics asks which angles reach a target. The arm can reach an annulus from 0.15 to 1.05 metres, further restricted by its joint limits.

02

Control asks “how?”

The PD controller produces torque from position error and angular velocity. Genesis advances the actual joint dynamics. Inertia, payload, damping and torque limits shape the response.

03

A model has a boundary.

This is a fixed-base horizontal two-link arm. The preview ignores dynamics. The Genesis run includes link inertia and actuator limits, with no environment obstacles, grasping, learning policy, or real hardware validation.
