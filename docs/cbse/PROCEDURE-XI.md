# Class XI guided apparatus modules

`experience/procedure-xi.js` registers all 34 standard Class XI rows already defined by the practical catalogue: `p11-a-e01…10`, `p11-a-a01…07`, `p11-b-e01…10` and `p11-b-a01…07`. Accessible alternatives remain linked preparation entries rather than being counted again as new experiments.

Each module has four ordered apparatus operations, corresponding SVG states, apparatus controls, virtual readings, a stated model and a formula or qualitative inference. The diagrams are original authored SVG schematics. They are not photographic evidence or a certification of physical laboratory skill.

## Coverage

- Measurement: external/internal/depth vernier operations; micrometer wire/sheet contact; lamina tracing and thickness; plane/spherical spherometer contact; equal-arm mass balance; paper-scale construction and uncertainty graphing.
- Mechanics: concurrent force board; length-period and constant-size variable-mass pendulums; limiting sliding friction and rolling threshold; inclined-plane holding force; moments; projectile range; double ramps; damped-pendulum energy proxy.
- Elasticity: differential wire-extension arrangement, spring and two explicitly different metre-rule support geometries.
- Fluids: non-mercury piston implementation of Boyle’s relation, capillary rise and detergent comparison, falling-sphere timing region and Reynolds-number check, equal-height Venturi pressure taps.
- Thermal: cooling, matched cooling comparison, calorimetry with container heat capacity, qualitative wax transition, bonded bimetal and liquid/container thermal lag with initial apparent level dip.
- Waves: both sonometer relations with fixed-wire assumptions, bridge adjustment and resonance cue; resonance-tube first/second positions with the water-surface displacement node.

Balance, moments, both friction thresholds and both sonometers expose recording validation. A separate demonstrated-setting operation transparently sets a valid example; arbitrary off-equilibrium or off-resonance values are not accepted as successful measurements.

## Numerical boundaries

Models use g = 9.81 m/s². Quantities, geometry and approximation limits are stated per module. The Young-modulus example uses 200 GPa. The capillary model uses near-zero contact angle and includes r/3. The falling-sphere model includes buoyancy and reports Reynolds number. Pendulum period uses the small-angle approximation. Sonometer readings identify the fundamental, not a higher mode. The two-resonance difference cancels a common end correction. Heat-loss multipliers, wax transition temperatures, rolling resistance and thermal-lag constants are selected illustrative scenarios, not fitted material properties.
