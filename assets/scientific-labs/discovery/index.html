◌

A little patience for the first discovery.

The browser notebook loads its Python runtime on first launch, which can take 10–120 seconds, and sometimes longer on a slow connection. Internet access is required for marimo and Pyodide assets. Uploaded CSVs are processed on your device. Reloading clears unsaved notes.

Prefer a local Python environment? The editable source, pinned requirements, launch commands and scientific limits are included.
